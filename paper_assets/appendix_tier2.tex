\subsection{Compute-matched comparison}
\label{app:compute_matched}

($T{=}8$, $\mathrm{max\_tok}{=}2048$) against $S^3$ at $k{=}2$
($T{=}16$, $\mathrm{max\_tok}{=}2048$) at the same total per-question

itself is pass-rate–limited compared to $k{=}4$: Naive@4 wins on
GAIA across every model and is competitive on HotpotQA and Bambogle,

\subsection{Pool size ablation}
\label{app:pool}

Table~\ref{tab:app-pool} sweeps the candidate pool size $n$ from
which the greedy max-min Jaccard selector picks $k{=}4$ seeds.
Pass@4 is essentially flat across $n \in \{4, 8, 16, 32\}$ in all
four cells (max spread $2.9$~pp on GAIA, where seed variance
dominates). The default $n{=}16$ used in the main results sits in
this flat region; bigger pools do not buy anything.

\subsection{Extent of diversification}
\label{app:extent}

Table~\ref{tab:app-extent} extends pool selection past turn~1.
$N{=}1$ is the default (turn-1-only diversity); $N{=}8$ overrides
every turn with a fresh diverse pool. Pass@4 changes by $\le 1.4$~pp
on HotpotQA and is noise-level on GAIA. Turn-1-only diversity
captures essentially all the gain --- consistent with Figure~1 of
the main paper, which shows turn-1 distance predicts turn-$T$
distance (cluster geometry is set at turn~1).

\subsection{Selection rule}
\label{app:selection}

Table~\ref{tab:app-selection} compares selectors on a single GAIA-103
cell (Qwen3-8B, $k{=}4$, single seed; gpt-4o-mini judge). Greedy
max-min Jaccard outperforms every MMR setting we tested on
pass@4 while simultaneously achieving the highest QPD and the
lowest ITC. We adopt greedy max-min Jaccard as the default
selector throughout the paper.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{6pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.05}
\begin{tabular}{l c c c}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & pass@4 (\%) & QPD~$\uparrow$ & ITC~$\downarrow$ \\
\midrule
\textbf{Greedy max-min Jaccard} & \textbf{34.0} & \textbf{0.861} & \textbf{0.266} \\
MMR ($\lambda{=}0.50$)          & 31.1 & 0.833 & 0.283 \\
MMR ($\lambda{=}0.75$)          & 30.1 & --- & --- \\
No selection (Naive)            & 28.2 & 0.372 & 0.461 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Selection rule ablation. Single-cell comparison on GAIA-103
with Qwen3-8B at $k{=}4$, single seed. Pass@4 is gpt-4o-mini-judged;
QPD is mean turn-1 pairwise Jaccard distance and ITC is mean
intra-thread coherence (\S3). Greedy max-min Jaccard wins on every
metric simultaneously. (MMR $\lambda{=}0.25$ tuned on a 30-question
subset only --- not run at full 103, hence omitted; uniform random
selector not run --- ``No selection'' is the closest baseline,
i.e., $k$ independent samples with no diversity pressure.)}
\label{tab:app-selection}
\end{table}
